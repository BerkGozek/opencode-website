Fullmetal Alchemist: Alchemy Basics

New to Fullmetal Alchemist? Alchemy is the science of changing matter, and it follows strict rules. Learn them here, then see how the story uses them.

The Rules of Alchemy

  1. Equivalent exchange. To gain something, you must give up something of equal value. Nothing is created from nothing.
  2. Transmutation circles. Most alchemists draw a circle to define what the transformation will do.
  3. Knowledge is power, and a cost. Skilled alchemists can transform matter quickly, but mistakes and forbidden acts carry a heavy price.

Alchemy in Action

  • Transmutation circle

    A drawn pattern that tells the alchemist what the change should do.

    Technique

  • Hand-clap transmutation

    Some alchemists clap their hands together to transform matter without drawing a circle.

    Technique

  • Automail

    Mechanical limbs that replace lost arms and legs, built with the help of skilled mechanics.

    Tool

  • State Alchemist watch

    A silver pocket watch that marks an alchemist as licensed by the military.

    Tool

  • Human transmutation

    Changing a human body is forbidden. Trying it always costs something, and it is the story's central mistake.

    Forbidden

  • Philosopher's Stone

    A legendary object that can break the rules of equivalent exchange, and its creation is forbidden.
